# Personalgeorgien

A German-language marketing landing page for employers seeking skilled workers from Georgia. Operates under the Personalgeorgien domain.

## Language

**Personalgeorgien**:
The domain and brand name for this employer-facing landing page. Written lowercase as one word: _personal_ (staff) in primary color, _georgien_ (Georgia) in black.

**Employer**:
A company in Germany that hires workers through this landing page.
_Avoid_: Client, customer

**Fachkraft**:
A skilled worker from Georgia who is available for employment in Germany.
_Avoid_: Arbeitskraft (broader term used elsewhere in the monorepo)

**Hero**:
The first section visitors see: headline and slogan on the left (60%), portrait video on the right (40%). On mobile, text appears above the video.

**Lead**:
An employer who contacts Personalgeorgien via the WhatsApp call-to-action.
_Avoid_: Inquiry, contact

**WhatsApp CTA**:
The primary call-to-action in the navbar and hero. Opens WhatsApp with a prefilled German message to the leads number +49 176 81376567.

**Vorteil**:
A concise reason for an employer to choose Personalgeorgien over other recruitment channels. Four are shown in the benefits section.
_Avoid_: Feature, perk

**Ablauf**:
The three-step process shown to employers: Kontakt, Auswahl, Einstellung.
_Avoid_: Workflow, pipeline

**Betreiber**:
Giorgi Sharashenidze, the person legally responsible for the Personalgeorgien website.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin

**Impressum**:
The legally required page identifying the site operator (Betreiber). Published at `/impressum`.
_Avoid_: About us, legal notice

**Datenschutz**:
The privacy policy page explaining how visitor data is handled. Published at `/datenschutz`. Covers WhatsApp redirects, server logs, and the absence of analytics cookies.
_Avoid_: Privacy policy (use the German term on this site)
